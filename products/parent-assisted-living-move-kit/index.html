About this printable

Moving a parent into assisted living is one of the hardest jobs a family takes on. The Gentle Transition Assisted Living Move Kit keeps the practical side organized, so you can focus on your parent.

A decision and timeline checklist walks you from the first conversation to move day. Fill-in scripts guide a family meeting with siblings and a first call to a community's admissions office. Bring the tour questions page on every visit, then score options side by side on the community comparison chart.

When it's time to pack, a room-by-room downsizing planner sorts belongings into bring, family, sell or donate, and a packing and move-day checklist covers familiar comforts and paperwork. A deadline tracker, a who-to-call contact sheet and a budget page that compares costs before and after the move, and a first-30-days page round it out.

Thirteen printable US Letter pages. General guidance only, not legal or financial advice.
