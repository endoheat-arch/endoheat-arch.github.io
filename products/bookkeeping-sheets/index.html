About this printable

Running a side hustle or a small shop and dreading the paperwork? The Clear Ledger Small Business Bookkeeping Kit keeps your numbers tidy on paper, with no software subscription needed.

Record each sale on the income log with customer, invoice number and payment method. Track costs on a matching expense log, organized by vendor and category with receipt numbers.
