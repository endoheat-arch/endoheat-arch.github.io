About this printable

Want kids who pitch in without daily reminders? The Happy Helpers Kids Chore Chart Set makes responsibility visible, colorful and genuinely rewarding.

A grown-ups page offers age-by-age chore ideas, from tidying toys at two to loading the dishwasher at eleven, plus tips for keeping charts motivating. Each child gets a weekly chart with space for chores and a column for every day. A family chore chart assigns jobs across the household, so everyone shares the work. Finally, a thirty-star reward tracker lets your child color a star for each success while working toward a prize you choose together.

Seven undated US Letter pages in one PDF. Hang them on the fridge, slide them into sheet protectors, or print fresh copies each week. Stickers work beautifully on the reward stars.
