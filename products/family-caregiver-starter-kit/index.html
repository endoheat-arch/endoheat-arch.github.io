Who it's for

Adults who have just started caring for a parent, partner or relative and need to get organized fast.

About this printable

When a parent or partner suddenly needs your help, everything seems to arrive at once: appointments, insurance questions, paperwork and the worry of fitting it all around work. The Steady Hands Family Caregiver Starter Kit gives you one calm binder to hold it together.

Begin with a first-days checklist, then record key details on a care snapshot page and a medication list copied straight from labels. An appointment log keeps track of what you were told at each visit. Three fill-in call scripts help you speak with a doctor's office, an insurer and your employer, with space to note names and reference numbers.

You'll also find a deadline and document tracker, a who-to-call contact sheet, a monthly caregiving budget, a weekly share-the-load schedule for family and friends, and a check-in page just for you.

Fourteen printable US Letter pages. It's an organizing tool for family caregivers, not medical or legal advice.
