About this printable

Owning a rental or two should not mean digging through texts, bank apps and shoeboxes every time rent is late, a repair comes up or taxes are due. The Small Landlord Rental Tracker puts every unit on paper in one binder.

Start with a property snapshot and unit roster, then log rent each month on the rent roll with dates, methods and late fees. A lease and deposit tracker with a return checklist helps you stay organized at move-out. Tenant contact cards, a maintenance and repair log and an expense tracker with deductible check-offs keep records ready for your tax preparer.

A vacancy and turnover checklist, move-in / move-out inspection sheet, year-at-a-glance calendar and notes finish the set.

You get 13 undated US Letter pages as a PDF (cover + 12 forms). Print one set per property or per year. Built for small and accidental landlords with 1–10 units. Not legal or tax advice.
