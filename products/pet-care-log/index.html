About this printable

Can you remember when your dog last had a booster, or how much your cat weighed at her last checkup? The Happy Paws Pet Care Log keeps those answers in one place.

The pet profile records breed, birthday, microchip number, food and vet contacts. Vaccination and medication tables show what is due next, while a vet visit log tracks reasons, weight and instructions from every appointment. A weekly care tracker lets the whole household tick off meals, water, walks, medication and cleanup, so nothing gets doubled or skipped.

Grooming notes, a supply shopping list and a weight chart help you spot changes early. When you travel, the pet sitter page hands over feeding times, hiding spots, house rules and emergency details.

This is an undated, 8-page US Letter PDF that works for dogs, cats, rabbits and other companions. Print a set for each pet. Keep copies handy for boarding or emergencies.
