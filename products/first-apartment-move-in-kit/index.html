About this printable

Getting the keys to your first apartment is exciting, and a little overwhelming. The Fresh Keys First Apartment Move-In Kit turns that pile of to-dos into calm, simple pages you can work through one at a time.

Start with a six-week countdown, then record the unit's condition on a room-by-room inspection sheet before you unpack a single box. Fill-in phone scripts give you the words for calls with your landlord and utility companies, so you know what to ask and can write down the answers. A setup tracker logs every provider, account number and deposit.

A deadline and document tracker keeps your lease, deposit receipt and renewal dates in view, and a who-to-call sheet holds every important number. Two budget pages cover one-time move-in costs and your new monthly spending.

You'll get 13 printable US Letter pages. It's a general organizing tool for renters, not legal advice. Perfect for students and new grads.
