How Digital Downloads Work on Payhip: Uploads, Instant Delivery, What Buyers Get and Refund Basics

Originally published on Telegraph

Selling a printable is different from print-on-demand: the product is the file. Payhip's Help Center spells out the path in plain steps — upload, purchase, download page, email receipt, and (if needed) a refund through PayPal or Stripe rather than by Payhip itself.

Numbers and rules below come from those official articles in Sources. Features change, so open the linked pages when you set something up.

General information only — not financial, legal or tax advice, and not a promise of income.

Why the delivery flow matters

If you sell a PDF planner or other download, delivery is mostly automatic. Payhip describes itself as a storefront where you add products, connect PayPal or Stripe, and customize branding; the platform handles the digital delivery path (How Payhip Works).

You do not manually email a file after every sale. When something goes wrong — a missing receipt, a download limit, a refund request — you still need to know what Payhip automates versus what stays on you as the seller.

Uploading files: formats, size limits and product setup

Payhip's guide to adding a digital product walks through the listing steps (Add Digital Products):

1. On the Products page, choose Add new product, then Digital Product 2. Upload with Upload product file — multiple files are allowed (ebooks, PDFs, audio, video and other types) 3. Each file can be up to 5GB; larger work can be split across multiple files on the same listing 4. Add a title, price, optional cover image, optional embedded media (YouTube, Vimeo or SoundCloud), and a description that says what the buyer gets

Supported types include PDFs, audio, video, ZIP files and more. For security, Payhip does not support direct uploads of EXE, ISO, DMG, VBS, SCR or JAR; those go inside a ZIP instead (Add Digital Products).

Useful options on the same page include visibility (Visible, Invisible, or Unlisted), a preview file, variations for formats or bundles, and a sale-quantity cap. You can edit files, price and description after publishing. To test checkout, Payhip suggests a 100% off coupon; test orders still appear on Customers and cannot be deleted (Add Digital Products).

Instant delivery: what happens the moment someone pays

For digital products, Payhip documents a four-step customer path (How Payhip Works):

1. The customer opens your product page and completes purchase 2. Payhip sends them to a download page so they can get the files right away 3. Payhip emails a receipt that includes the download-page link for later 4. Payment goes to your connected PayPal or Stripe account, and Payhip emails you a sale notification

The buyer-facing help article matches that story: after a successful payment, the shopper is shown or redirected to the download page, and a receipt goes to their email at the same time. Digital products are described as delivered instantly via that download page (Buying from Payhip).

You do not ship anything. After a clean sale, your job is mostly buyer questions and keeping files current.

What buyers actually receive

Payhip's buyer guide says a completed digital purchase includes immediate download-page access, an email receipt (order details, download or login link, seller contact email), and an option to generate an invoice from the receipt. Sellers see the buyer's email for support; they do not get full card details — payments run through Stripe or PayPal (Buying from Payhip).

If a buyer cannot find the files, Payhip's troubleshooting article lists common causes: missing receipt email, wrong checkout email, a deleted/replaced file on the listing, a still-pending or failed payment, a refunded or cancelled order, browser/device issues, or content that is login-based rather than a file download (Did Not Receive Files or Orders). When an order is refunded or cancelled, Payhip says access to digital files is revoked immediately (Did Not Receive Files or Orders).

Download limits and optional PDF stamping

Delivery is automatic, but sharing is not unlimited. Under Advanced Settings, Payhip lets you set a download limit per customer. The default is 5 downloads; you can change that number, and you can reset a customer's credits if they hit the cap (Protect Your Products; Download Credits).

Optional PDF stamping puts the buyer's email and purchase date on the top left of every page of PDF files under 250MB (portrait PDFs only, as of Payhip's help note). That is meant to discourage casual file sharing (Protect Your Products).

Neither replaces a clear product description; both sit on top of instant delivery.

Refund basics for sellers and buyers

Payhip is explicit: it does not hold funds and cannot issue refunds directly. Buyers contact the seller. Each seller sets their own policy; some refund and some do not, especially after a download (Refund Request).

Before asking for money back, Payhip tells buyers to retry the download, switch browser or device, and re-read instructions. Contact routes are the seller email on the receipt and the store contact page. Reply times are often about 24 to 72 hours for small creators (Refund Request).

As a seller, you refund in PayPal or Stripe (PayPal Activity → Refund, or Stripe Payments → Refund payment, full or partial). Those refunds then appear in Payhip sales reports and analytics (How Do I Refund a Transaction?).

Publish your policy where buyers can find it, and answer mail from the address on your receipts. The file arrives fast; support still lands on you.

A simple checklist before you hit publish

1. Upload the final files (or a ZIP), confirm the 5GB-per-file limit, and write a description that lists what is inside (Add Digital Products). 2. Connect PayPal or Stripe so checkout and deposits work (How Payhip Works). 3. Run a 100% off coupon test so you see the real download page and receipt (Add Digital Products). 4. Set download limit / PDF stamp if you want those protections (Protect Your Products). 5. Publish a refund policy your shoppers can find, knowing refunds run through PayPal or Stripe and that digital access ends when an order is refunded (Refund Request; How Do I Refund a Transaction?; Did Not Receive Files or Orders).
