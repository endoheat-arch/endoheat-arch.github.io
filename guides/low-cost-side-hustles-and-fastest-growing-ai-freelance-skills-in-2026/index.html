According to Bankrate’s June 2025 survey of 2,616 U.S. adults, 27% currently have a side hustle, down from 36% in 2024. Side hustlers reported average monthly earnings of $885, with a median of $200. (Bankrate)

This drop does not mean side income is obsolete. Bankrate’s senior industry analyst attributes it to a strong job market and a cooling inflation rate, and notes that side hustling could rise again if employment trends weaken. For those who still want or need extra cash—especially with low or zero startup costs—several paths remain practical.

Upwork’s In-Demand Skills 2026 report (released February 2026, based on 2025 U.S. marketplace completed jobs) shows skills explicitly tied to applying AI grew 109% year-over-year. The fastest-growing included AI video generation and editing (+329%), AI integration (+178%), AI data annotation and labeling (+154%), and AI chatbot development (+71%). (Upwork)

These are applied skills layered onto existing work categories (video, development, data, support) rather than entirely new professions. Demand for human expertise alongside AI tools remains strong.

Low-startup options include:

  • Digital products (printables, templates, planners) on platforms like Etsy or Payhip: create once, sell repeatedly. Startup cost near zero beyond design tools.
  • Freelance services on Upwork or similar, starting with skills you already have and adding AI tools for efficiency.
  • Selling unused items or simple local services with no inventory.

Median earnings remain modest for most, so treat early results as validation rather than a full replacement for other income. Track expenses and report self-employment income as required.
