High-Paying Side Hustles in 2026: Real Rates from Forbes, Upwork, and Bankrate

Originally published on Telegraph

  • Conclusion

Side hustles remain a practical way for many people to supplement income, even as overall participation has shifted. According to Bankrate's June 2025 survey of 2,616 U.S. adults, 27% currently have a side hustle—the lowest share since 2017 and down from 36% in 2024. The median monthly earnings are $200, while the average is $885, pulled higher by a smaller group of stronger earners. Thirty-five percent of side hustlers use at least some of the extra income for regular living expenses. [Bankrate Side Hustle Survey, July 2025]

Among those who do earn extra, skill-based work often outperforms pure gig delivery. Forbes contributor Bryan Robinson highlighted NetCredit's 2026 study of Fiverr rates for a single day's work. Six categories tied for the top median price of $100 per 24-hour assignment: AI spokesperson videos, business planning, AI applications, applications and registration, marketing strategy, and generative AI lessons. AI-related services appeared prominently, with AI spokesperson videos holding a top spot even as its median slipped from $110 in 2025. Resume writing and career writing became the top-paying writing gigs at $95. [NetCredit, September 2026; Forbes, September 22, 2026]

Separate analyses of marketplace rates reinforce the value of specialized skills. On Upwork, business coaches typically charge $75 to $150 per hour and AI developers $30 to $150 per hour, according to Upwork's own rate pages, which reflect historical contracts worldwide. AI video generation and editing was among the fastest-growing skills on Upwork in 2025, with freelancer earnings in that category up 329%. [Upwork rate pages; Upwork In-Demand Skills 2026]

Tutoring, teaching, or coaching stands out in other datasets as a high earner, with one Integra Credit survey citing average annual side income of $19,200 for people in those fields. Professional services and specialized freelancing (copywriting, AI prompt or application work) frequently appear in "highest-paying" lists because they leverage existing expertise rather than pure time-for-money delivery apps. [Integra Credit; Forbes, August 17, 2026; Forbes, March 5, 2026]

Practical starting points that align with current data:

  • Inventory a skill you already have (writing, design, analysis, teaching, AI tool use) and list it on Upwork, Fiverr, or via direct outreach.
  • For AI-related work, focus on specific deliverables such as custom workflows, spokesperson videos, or integration rather than generic "AI help."
  • Track real payouts and fees; many delivery and survey options net lower after costs and unpaid time.
  • If your net earnings from self-employment reach $400 or more in a year, you have to file a federal tax return (IRS).

These figures come from published surveys, marketplace rate data, and company research as of mid-to-late 2026. Individual results vary widely with skill level, location, marketing, and consistency. Treat them as benchmarks, not guarantees.
